Troubleshooting

My message stays "Queued"

  • Check the connection indicator: are any relays connected? Open Settings → Relays to see each relay's state.
  • Tor strict mode blocks all relays until Tor is confirmed. See Tor.
  • A relay may require authentication (NIP-42) or reject your traffic. Add another relay.
  • Queued messages are sent automatically when a relay is reachable.

"Sent to relays" but my contact gets nothing

  • Your contact's app must be open (or opened later) to fetch messages.
  • You may have added them by npub only. Vexunori does not look up published relay lists (NIP-17 kind 10050), so it is sending to your relays, which they may not read. Ask for their invite link, which includes their inbox relays.
  • Their message requests may be off, so messages from you are dropped until they add you.
  • They may have blocked you, or the relay may have deleted the events.

Old messages are missing on a linked device

A new device only sees what your relays still keep. Public relays may delete old events. Use a full backup to move complete history.

Attachment stuck at "receiving"

Each attachment arrives as many small encrypted pieces. Some may be delayed or dropped by a rate-limited relay. Wait, or ask the sender to resend. A file that failed its checksum is shown as an error and never opened.

The APK will not install

  • Allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps (see the install guide).
  • "App not installed" over an existing version usually means a different signing key (for example an APK from somewhere other than this website). Uninstalling deletes your data — back up first.
  • Android 7.0 or newer is required.

Data disappeared in the browser

Browsers can delete site storage (private windows, "clear data on exit", storage pressure, long inactivity on some browsers). Vexunori cannot prevent this. Keep a backup.

I forgot my passphrase

It cannot be reset. Restore from a backup or recovery shares on a fresh install. See If you lose….

No notifications

Vexunori only notifies while it is running and after you enable notifications and grant permission. On Android, battery optimisation may stop the app in the background. See Notifications.

Calls do not connect

Calls need a direct path between devices; there is no TURN relay. Try both devices on the same Wi-Fi, or add a STUN server. Calls are disabled in Tor strict mode.

Still stuck?

Check the limits listed on the Features page and under known limits. Security problems: see how to report. When reporting other bugs, never include your secret key, passphrase or message content.
