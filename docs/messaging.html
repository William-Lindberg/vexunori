Messaging

Every message is encrypted on your device for each recipient using the open NIP-17 standard: the message is encrypted (NIP-44 v2) and signed inside a seal, which is encrypted again inside a gift wrap signed by a random one-time key. Relays store the gift wraps until the recipient fetches them.

Message statuses

Vexunori only shows a status when the event really happened. A check mark is never shown because "it probably arrived".

StatusWhat it means
QueuedSaved on your device, not yet accepted by any relay (you are offline, relays are unreachable, or Tor strict mode is blocking). It is sent automatically when a connection is possible.
SendingBeing published right now.
Sent to relaysAt least one relay answered "OK" for every recipient's copy. This is not proof that anyone received it. The message info dialog lists which relays accepted it.
DeliveredThe recipient's app sent back an encrypted delivery receipt. Only shown if their delivery receipts are on (the default).
ReadThe recipient's app sent a read receipt. Read receipts are off by default; you only see them from people who turned them on.
FailedEvery relay rejected or timed out. Vexunori keeps retrying; you can also retry by hand.

In groups, delivered and read show per member in the message info. No receipts are ever sent to people in your message requests.

Edits and deletes

  • Edit: you can edit your own messages. Recipients' apps replace the text and keep the earlier versions visible in the message info. People who already read or copied the old text still have it.
  • Delete for everyone: sends a request to every participant's app to remove the message. Vexunori apps honour it and keep a "message deleted" placeholder. A modified app, a screenshot or a copy cannot be undone, and relays may keep the encrypted envelope.
  • Delete for me: removes the message from this device only.

Disappearing messages

A conversation timer (5 minutes to 4 weeks) is shared with everyone in the conversation. Each Vexunori app deletes messages locally once the timer has passed since sending. The envelope also carries an expiration hint (NIP-40) that relays may honour.

  • It does not stop screenshots, copies or a modified app.
  • A device that is off deletes the message when it next runs.
  • Relays that ignore the hint keep the encrypted envelope; without forward secrecy, it stays decryptable for whoever later obtains the key.

Attachments

  • Up to 3 files per message, each up to 1.5 MB.
  • Files are split into encrypted chunks, each sent like a message. The recipient's app reassembles them and only shows the file after its SHA-256 matches. A file that fails verification is shown as an error, not opened.
  • Before sending, SendGuard shows the file's type and metadata (for example photo location) and lets you send a stripped copy.
  • The limits are small on purpose: public relays rate-limit bursts of large events. For larger files, use a parcel or another channel.

Replies, reactions, search, pins

Reply to a message to quote it; react with an emoji (reactions are encrypted like messages). Pin important messages in a conversation, bookmark messages for yourself, and keep drafts. Search runs only on your device over the messages it has decrypted; nothing is sent to a server. Search ignores letter case and accents.

Typing indicators are not implemented: the other person cannot see when you are typing, and you cannot see when they are.

Relays and delivery

Your messages go to the recipient's inbox relays (from their invite) and a copy to your own relays so your other devices see them. Vexunori does not look up published relay lists (NIP-17 kind 10050): for a contact added by npub only, it sends to your own relays, which they may not read. By default Vexunori uses four public community relays. They are run by volunteers, are free, have no service guarantee and may change their rules. You can change relays in Settings → Relays or run your own.

If the recipient's relays are down, messages wait there or in your queue. If a relay deletes old events before the recipient comes online, that message is lost; the sender sees "Sent to relays" but never "Delivered".
