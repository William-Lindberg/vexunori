Self-hosting

You do not need to run anything to use Vexunori. Self-hosting is optional.

Your own relay

A relay of your own gives you an inbox relay you control, a relay for your family or community, or a relay on your local network. There are two ways to get one:

  • Any standard Nostr relay that accepts private-message gift wraps (kind 1059) from your devices. Vexunori talks to relays only through the open Nostr protocol.
  • The small Vexunori relay (Node.js, one process, no database). It is not published: Vexunori is not open source, and the licence allows running the Vexunori relay software only if you received it from the copyright holder for that purpose. Its setup guide (TLS with a reverse proxy, systemd, retention, what the operator can see, costs) comes with it.

Then add your relay in Settings → Relays (a wss:// address; a plain ws:// address only for a relay on your local network, see LAN relay). Share a new invite so contacts learn your new inbox relay: Vexunori does not publish or look up relay lists (NIP-17 kind 10050), so the invite is how contacts find it.

Running a relay makes you the operator who sees recipient keys, timing and IP addresses (see Privacy). Your hardware at home costs only electricity; a rented server costs money and is not required.

The website and web app

The website and the web app are hosted only by the project. The licence does not allow copying or re-hosting them, so there is no self-hosted web app.
