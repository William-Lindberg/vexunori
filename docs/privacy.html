Privacy & data handling

Vexunori has no accounts, no central server holding your data, no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising and no AI services. This page explains where your data goes when you use it.

Stays on your device

  • Your secret key, contacts, messages (decrypted), Vault, Spaces, workspace data, drafts, settings, privacy receipts and Passports.
  • All of it is encrypted at rest (see key modes). Search runs locally.
  • Theme choice is additionally stored unencrypted in the browser's local storage so the lock screen can use it.

Sent to relays

Relays receive encrypted gift wraps. A relay operator can see:

  • the recipient's public key on every envelope;
  • your IP address (unless you use Tor on Android);
  • when envelopes arrive and are fetched, and their sizes (the time written inside is randomised up to two days back, the arrival time is not);
  • your public key when your own relay asks you to authenticate (NIP-42) before handing over your inbox, which links it to your IP address. Vexunori only authenticates to your own relays, never to a recipient's.

Vexunori does not publish a public profile or relay list; your invite link carries that information instead. The operator cannot see the content or the sender of an envelope. However, when you send a message Vexunori also sends a copy to yourself; if you and your recipient use the same relay, the operator can see two envelopes arrive together from your IP address and infer who wrote to whom. Relays decide how long they keep data.

Seen by others

  • Recipients see what you send, your public key and display name, and your receipts if enabled. In a call, the other person sees your IP address.
  • Network observers see that you connect to relays and how much data you exchange.
  • This website's host sees normal web server logs when you load pages, the web app or the APK. The site sets no cookies and loads nothing from third parties.
  • Optional services you turn on: Tor check services (only in Tor mode or when you press "Check now"), STUN servers you add for calls.

Never collected

Phone numbers, email addresses, contact lists, usage statistics, crash reports, device identifiers or location. Nothing about you is sent to the Vexunori project.

Panic: freeze quickly

Emergency Freeze locks Vexunori at once, drops keys and decrypted data from memory and disconnects from relays. Nothing is deleted. Besides the button in the Privacy Center there are two quick ways to trigger it:

  • Shake to freeze (off by default, Settings → Security): shake the phone hard three times within 1.5 seconds. Choose the sensitivity with the slider and try it with “Test shake detection”, which counts shakes without freezing. It only works while Vexunori is open and in front, and needs a motion sensor; some browsers ask for permission or do not report motion at all. A high sensitivity may freeze by accident, for example while running.
  • Quick Settings tile (Android): add the “Quick lock” tile to your Quick Settings (pull down the notification shade, tap edit, drag the tile in). Tapping it freezes a running Vexunori immediately; if Vexunori was closed, it opens frozen the next time.

Pressing the power button several times is not offered: Android does not let apps detect it.

Disguise the app

In Settings → Appearance → Disguise you can make Vexunori look like an ordinary app: Calculator, Notes, Weather, Clock, Calendar or Flashlight. The icons are simple, generic drawings made for Vexunori.

  • Android: the icon and name in your launcher change. Launchers can take a few seconds to refresh, and some keep an old home-screen shortcut until you remove it. The real name still shows in Android Settings → Apps, in notifications and in the share sheet, and the start-up splash screen still shows the Vexunori logo.
  • Web app: a web page cannot change its icon or name, so this is called Disguise mode: only the decoy screen changes. The browser still shows the address, history and the installed app's name.
  • The app opens on a working decoy: a real calculator, simple notes, a clock, a month calendar, a screen light, or a weather screen that shows “No data” (it has no data source). To reach the real lock screen, type your secret code on the calculator and press “=”; on the other screens press and hold the title, then enter the code. The decoy comes back whenever Vexunori locks or restarts.
  • This is not a security feature. The 4–12 digit code is stored hashed (SHA-256 with a random salt) but unencrypted on the device, because it must work before unlock. Someone who copies the app's storage can guess a short code quickly. Your messages stay protected by the lock screen and encryption. Notes typed into the Notes decoy are stored unencrypted.
  • Forgot the code? Press and hold the title and enter your app passphrase instead. Without a passphrase (Keystore or device mode) the only way back is clearing the app's data, which deletes everything on the device.
  • Turn off notification previews too (Settings → Notifications), or a message notification may give the app away.

Deleting data

  • You can delete messages, conversations, Vault items, receipts and drafts, set automatic retention, or wipe everything in Settings.
  • Local deletion does not reach other people's devices, their backups, or relays that already stored envelopes.
