Contacts & verification

Adding contacts

You can add someone with:

  • an invite link (it opens Vexunori and contains their public key, display name and inbox relays);
  • their QR code, scanned with your camera;
  • their npub or nprofile, pasted. An npub alone has no relay information. Vexunori does not look up the relay list people may publish (NIP-17 kind 10050), so it sends to your own relays, and the other person may never receive your messages unless they also read from one of them. Prefer an invite link or QR code.

Invites contain nothing secret, but anyone who sees an invite can write to you. Share it the way you would share a phone number.

Message requests and blocking

Messages from keys that are not your contacts land in Requests. They do not get delivery or read receipts. You can accept, delete or block. If you turn requests off in Settings, messages from unknown keys are dropped.

Blocking drops everything from that key before it is stored. The other person is not told. Because anyone can make new keys for free, blocking cannot stop a determined sender who changes keys.

Removing a contact keeps your history unless you choose to delete it too.

Safety numbers

When an invite is passed through someone else (a chat group, an email, a website), they could swap in their own key and read your messages. Safety numbers let you check that did not happen.

  1. Open the contact and choose Verify.
  2. Compare the 60-digit number with the one on your contact's screen — in person, or on a video call where you recognise each other. Or scan their verification QR code.
  3. If they match, mark the contact as verified.

The number is computed from both public keys (iterated SHA-512), so both of you see the same digits.

What it proves: you each hold the other's real public key. What it does not prove: who controls the device that holds that key, or that their device is not compromised. Comparing numbers over the same channel an attacker could control (for example, sending them in the chat you want to verify) proves nothing.

If a contact starts using a new key, it is a different contact to Vexunori; verify the new one again.
