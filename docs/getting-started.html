Getting started

Vexunori runs in your web browser and as an Android app. Both are the same app with the same features, except where a guide says otherwise.

Use the web app

  1. Open the web app in a current browser (Firefox, Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Tor Browser).
  2. Choose Create a new identity, or restore one from a backup or another device.
  3. Choose how to protect your data on this device (see below).

You can install the web app like an app ("Install" or "Add to Home Screen" in your browser menu). Your data stays in this browser's storage for this site. Clearing site data deletes it — make a backup.

Private/incognito windows delete their storage when closed, and some browsers delete site storage after a period of no use. Do not keep your only copy of an identity in such a browser.

Install on Android

Download the APK from the download page, check its checksum, and allow your browser to install unknown apps. The download page walks you through it. Vexunori is not on Google Play. Version 0.1.0 of the Android app has been built and checked by automated tests but not yet run on a phone, so expect problems.

Protect your data on this device

Passphrase (recommended)
You type a passphrase to unlock the app. Your local data cannot be read without it. If you forget it and have no backup, your data is gone.
Android Keystore (Android only)
The app unlocks without typing, using a key the phone keeps in its secure hardware. Protects against copying the app's files off the phone, not against someone who can unlock and use your phone.
No passphrase (web only)
For trying the app. Provides no protection at rest: anyone with access to this browser profile can read your data. You can add a passphrase later in Settings.

Send your first message

  1. Go to Contacts → Add and share your invite link or show your QR code.
  2. Your friend opens the link (or scans the code) in their Vexunori and adds you; you add them the same way, or accept their message request.
  3. Open the chat and write. The small status under your message shows whether relays accepted it (what the statuses mean).
  4. When you can, compare safety numbers in person.
