Nearby & offline

When there is no internet, Vexunori can hand over messages that are already encrypted in three ways. None of them decrypts anything on the way.

QR parcels

On the Nearby screen, choose a contact and Send parcel. Vexunori bundles the encrypted envelopes waiting for that contact and shows them as a QR code — or an animated sequence of QR codes if it does not fit in one. The other person opens Receive parcel and points their camera at your screen. Their app imports the envelopes it can open and ignores duplicates.

  • Range: as far as a camera can read a screen.
  • Best for text messages; attachments make parcels much longer.
  • Whoever sees the QR codes can see the recipient's public key, how many envelopes there are and their sizes — not the content.

Parcel files

The same parcel can be saved as a .vxparcel file and shared through your phone's share sheet: Bluetooth, Nearby/Quick Share, a USB stick, email. Vexunori does not drive Bluetooth itself; it uses whatever your operating system offers. The receiver imports the file on the Nearby screen.

LAN relay

If one device on the local network (for example a laptop) runs a relay, phones on the same Wi-Fi can use it like any other relay, without internet access:

  1. Start a relay on the laptop, listening on the local network (port 7447 in the example below). Any Nostr relay that accepts kind 1059 gift wraps works; see Self-hosting.
  2. In Vexunori, open Nearby & offline and go to the LAN relay section. Turn on Allow LAN relays (plain ws:// is then allowed, but only for local network addresses), type the address in LAN relay address, for example ws://192.168.1.20:7447, and tap Add.
  3. Everyone who should be reachable adds the same LAN relay, then shares a fresh invite so the others know they also read from it.

Without TLS, anyone on the same network can see the envelopes and recipient keys travelling to the relay (still not the content).

  • Browsers block plain ws:// connections from the web app, which is loaded over https://. Use the Android app for a ws:// LAN relay, or a wss:// address with a certificate your devices trust.
  • LAN relays cannot be reached through Tor; they stay blocked while Tor mode is on.
  • Guest Wi-Fi and some public networks keep devices apart; a LAN relay will not work there. A phone hotspot usually works.

What is not available

  • There is no Bluetooth or Wi-Fi Direct mesh and no automatic discovery of nearby people. The Nearby screen shows it as "Not available in this version".
  • Nothing happens in the background: both people must have the app open for a QR hand-over.
  • The Nearby screen shows what was actually sent and imported; there is no "probably delivered".
