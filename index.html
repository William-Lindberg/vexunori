Private by design. Connected by choice.

Message, collect and work together — without an account.

Vexunori is a free-to-use messenger and collaboration app. Your identity is a key made on your own device. Messages are end-to-end encrypted and carried by relays that cannot read them. Everything you keep is encrypted on your device. No phone number, no email, no trackers.

One place from first link to shared result

Vexunori is built around a simple journey. Each step happens on your device, and every move between steps is shown to you before it happens.

  1. BrowseOpen links outside the app after the link inspector shows where they really go.
  2. SaveKeep notes, links and files in your encrypted Vault.
  3. InspectSendGuard shows file types, names and metadata such as photo location before anything leaves.
  4. DiscussTalk it through in end-to-end encrypted chats and groups.
  5. OrganizeSort everything into Spaces and shared group workspaces.
  6. ShareSend to people, export, or hand over offline by QR code — with a local record of what you did.

One-to-one chats and groups of up to 100, using the open NIP-17 standard: NIP-44 encryption inside gift wraps. Replies, reactions, edits, attachments up to 1.5 MB.

How it works

  1. Create a key

    Vexunori makes a key pair on your device and encrypts your local data with a passphrase (or the Android Keystore).

  2. Share an invite

    Send a link or show a QR code. It contains your public key, name and inbox relays — nothing secret.

  3. Messages are sealed

    Each message is encrypted for each recipient, signed inside, then wrapped with a one-time key that hides who sent it.

  4. Relays deliver

    Community relays store the sealed envelopes until the recipient's app fetches them. Relays cannot read them, but they do see some metadata.

What Vexunori does not do

Being clear about limits is part of the design.

  • It does not make you anonymous. Relays see which public key receives messages, when, and from which IP address.
  • It has no forward secrecy. If your secret key leaks, stored past messages can be decrypted.
  • It has not been externally audited. It uses established open-source libraries for its cryptography, but the app itself has had no independent review.
  • It is not open source. It is free to use, but its source code is not public, so nobody outside the project can currently review it. See the licence page.
  • It cannot recover your account without your passphrase, a backup or recovery shares. There is no server copy.
  • It cannot force deletion on other people's devices; delete-for-everyone is a request their app honours.
  • It sends no push notifications. You are notified only while the app is running.
  • It has no Bluetooth or Wi-Fi Direct mesh. Offline hand-over uses QR codes, files and local relays.
  • It has no AI features, no analytics, no ads and no cookies.
  • It is not on Google Play. The Android app is installed from this website. It has been build-checked only: it has not yet been tested on a phone or an emulator.
